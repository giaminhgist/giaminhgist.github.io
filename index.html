About

I am interested in using artificial intelligence and computational methods to address critical challenges in the medical field, especially early disease classification and domain adaptation. I also have experience in medical system design using LABVIEW, including ultrasound stimulation systems and real-time multi-sensor data acquisition platforms.
Key Interests:
  • Foundation models for medical image classification.
  • Vision-language models for medical report retrieval.
  • Domain adaptation across diverse medical cohorts.
  • Medical system design for real-time data acquisition and control.
  • Digital Twin for Organ Transplantation.
